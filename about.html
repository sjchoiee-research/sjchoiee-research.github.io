Sung-Jin Choi.

Professor · School of Electrical Engineering · Kookmin University

최성진 · 국민대학교 전자공학부

I am a professor at Kookmin University, where I study semiconductor devices and their integration into electronic systems. My research spans silicon transistors, carbon nanotube electronics, memory and neuromorphic devices, and semiconductor sensors. Within the shared S!LK laboratory, my team connects device design, fabrication and electrical characterization to questions in energy-efficient and densely integrated electronics. I joined Kookmin in 2013 following postdoctoral research at the University of California, Berkeley, and received my Ph.D. in electrical engineering from KAIST.
